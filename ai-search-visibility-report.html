AI Search VisibilityDo they name you at all?

We ask ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and Perplexity who to hire in your trade and your city, and count how often your business appears in the answers. If you are not named, buyers never see you.

Machine-Readable PricingCan the tools quote your prices?

Google Business Profile StrengthGraded against the top three

Your profile, graded against the three competitors the AI tools name instead of you. A weak or missing profile is one of the most common reasons a business stays invisible.

Services and Service-Area ClarityCan a machine tell what you do?

Your review count and rating, measured against the same three competitors. The tools lean on reviews when they decide who sounds safe to recommend.

Phone, email, and a working way to ask for work. Being named does you no good if the buyer who finds you cannot get through.
