Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026 · VonLloyd, LLC dba Kestrel Metrics

Kestrel Metrics is a service of VonLloyd, LLC ("Kestrel Metrics," "we," "us," or "our"). This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to our website (kestrelmetrics.com), our advertising forms, our reports, and our newsletter.

Information we collect

Information you give us. When you request a report, book a call, subscribe to our newsletter, or contact us, we collect the details you provide: your name, email address, phone number, business name, business website, type of business, city and state, and the revenue range you select. If you become a client, we also collect the business information needed to perform the work, such as access to the reports and records you choose to share with us.

Information collected automatically. When you visit our website or interact with our ads, we and our advertising partners may collect device and usage information through cookies and similar technologies, including pages visited, ads viewed or clicked, and general location (city/state level). Meta (Facebook and Instagram) provides advertising measurement tools, including the Meta Pixel, that work this way.

Information from public sources. Our reports are built from publicly available information about your business, such as your website, public listings, and published reviews. We do not collect private data about you from third parties.

How we use your information

  • To prepare and deliver the report or service you requested
  • To book and conduct calls you schedule with us
  • To respond to your questions and provide support
  • To send the newsletter, but only if you opted in to receive it
  • To measure and improve our advertising, including showing our ads to people similar to those who requested reports
  • To meet legal, accounting, and security obligations

We do not sell your personal information.

How we share information

We share information only with the service providers who run our business systems, and only as needed for them to do their work: Meta (advertising and lead forms), GoHighLevel (customer records, forms, scheduling, and messages), Google (documents, email, and analytics), and beehiiv (newsletter delivery, for subscribers only). These providers process information under their own privacy policies and our instructions.

We may also share information when required by law, or to protect our rights, your safety, or the safety of others. If our business is ever sold or merged, information may transfer as part of that transaction.

Cookies and advertising

Our website and ads use cookies and the Meta Pixel to understand which ads lead to report requests and to reach similar business owners. You can control cookies in your browser settings. You can opt out of interest-based advertising through your Meta ad preferences and through industry opt-out pages such as aboutads.info.

Text messages and email

If you give us your phone number and agree to texts, we may send messages about your report or your appointment. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out of texts at any time. Every marketing email we send includes an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing always works.

Consent: You will only receive text messages from us if you have provided express consent, such as by checking the SMS consent box on our website form or providing your number and verbal consent during a call. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

Opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message. Reply HELP for help, or contact us at admin@kestrelmetrics.com.

No third-party sharing: Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with or sold to any third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

How long we keep information

We keep lead and client information for as long as it is useful for the purposes above or required by law, and we delete or anonymize it when it is no longer needed. You may ask us to delete your information at any time (see Your choices).

Your choices

You may ask us to show you the personal information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. You may opt out of marketing emails and texts at any time without affecting reports or services you already requested. To make any of these requests, email admin@kestrelmetrics.com and we will respond within a reasonable time, normally within 30 days.

Children

Our services are for business owners and operators. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to a business of our size, including account access controls on the systems listed above. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the updated version at this address and update the date at the top. Material changes will be announced to subscribers and clients by email.
